The pipeline.

Five stages, one Rust core, compiled twice. Nothing is approximated twice.

Stage 01 · parse

The container opens, nothing is thrown away

OPC is unzipped entry by entry. Every part — even ones Quire has never seen — is claimed, content-addressed, held for write-back. Unknown is not the same as absent.

Stage 02 · shape + break

Real metrics, Knuth–Plass optimum

Shaped advances against real fonts; breaks chosen jointly per paragraph to minimise total badness. UAX#14 decides legality — KP decides what is right.

Stage 03 · paginate

One keystroke ≠ re-paginate the chapter

Paragraph caches reflow only the dirty interval; breaks settle in background passes. p95 incremental reflow under 8 ms. Output: a display list both renderers draw from.

無損不是承諾，而是結構：一個 Office 檔案是數千個宣告的容器，引擎未觸碰的每一個宣告、每一條 ZIP entry、每一個位元組，都會在寫回時原樣出現。瀏覽器分頁與桌面共用同一個排版核心：相同的整形 advance、相同的最佳化斷點、相同的 display list。沒有任何計算被近似兩次，因此也不可能出現分歧——你現在看到的這一行，與原生端渲染的是同一個答案。
